# Agentic Organization Structure and Roles

At Fab Agent, we are transitioning from a classical organization structure to an **agentic organization** structure. In this article, we explain roles, responsibilities, and the agent-personnel relationship.

## Why an Agentic Structure?

In traditional organizations, every employee has their own tools and processes. In an agentic structure:

- Each personnel works with one or more **agents** assigned to them.
- Agents take over the personnel's repetitive tasks.
- Personnel train, evaluate, and develop their agents.

## Roles

| Role                   | Responsibilities                                              |
|------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Organization Admin** | Manages Agents, Skills, Policies + MCP approvals             |
| **Department Lead**    | Manages agents and policies within their own department       |
| **Personnel**          | Works with agents, develops skills, evaluates outcomes        |
| **Agent**              | Operates autonomously using defined skills and policies       |

## Agent-Personnel Relationship

On the platform, each agent is assigned to **exactly one personnel member**. In this relationship:

- The agent's outputs are the responsibility of the personnel.
- Personnel evaluate the results produced by the agent.
- Development requests flow from personnel to admin.

This structure both clarifies accountability and ensures that agents operate safely and in a controlled manner.
