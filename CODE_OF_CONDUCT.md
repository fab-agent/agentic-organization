# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for everyone who
contributes, reports issues or asks questions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical where possible.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's personal information, and do not
  paste customer or company data into issues, pull requests or logs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Doxxing, sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unsolicited contact.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages) and to public spaces when someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to **bilgi@kuntaykunt.com**. Maintainers will
review reports promptly and confidentially, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants, and may edit or remove contributions that violate
this code. Security vulnerabilities are handled separately, see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
